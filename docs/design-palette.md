# Design → Palette

Controls the colours assigned to the chart's series, or to the slices of a pie or donut chart. A palette is a named InDesign colour group.

---

## Select Palette

- **Chart Palette**: the colour group applied to the chart. Series or slices are coloured by cycling through its swatches. Click **↺** to reread the colour groups from the document.
- **Preview Current Chart Palette**: shows one colour patch per series or slice, so you can see which colour each one gets.

To give a single series a different colour, use **Override color** on [Design → Series → Lines](design-series-lines.md#line-color).

---

## Convert Palette to Color Group

Adds the selected palette to the document as a new InDesign colour group and assigns it to the chart.

- **Modification**
  - *Copy Regular Palette*: adds the palette as it is.
  - *Create Gradient Palette*: creates colours that blend smoothly from the palette's first to its last swatch, one for each series or slice.
  - *Create Tint/Shade Palette*: creates lighter and darker variations of the palette's colours.
- **Preview New Chart Palette**: shows the colours that will be created.
- **New Color Group Name**: the name of the new colour group.

Click **Create & Assign Color Group** to add the colour group to the document and use it for the chart.
