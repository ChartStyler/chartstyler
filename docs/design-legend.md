# Design → Legend

Controls whether a legend is shown and how it is positioned and styled.

---

## Show Legend

Toggles the legend on or off. All other legend options are hidden when the legend is disabled.

---

## Legend Title

Optional text displayed at the top of the legend. Leave blank to show no title.

---

## Legend Position

Where the legend is placed relative to the chart frame. Options:

| Group | Values |
|---|---|
| Right | Right-Top, Right-Middle, Right-Bottom |
| Left | Left-Top, Left-Middle, Left-Bottom |
| Top | Top-Left, Top-Center, Top-Right |
| Bottom | Bottom-Left, Bottom-Center, Bottom-Right |
| Inside | Inside-Top-Left, Inside-Top-Center, Inside-Top-Right, Inside-Bottom-Left, Inside-Bottom-Center, Inside-Bottom-Right |

> **Tip:** After placing a chart, you can move the legend frame manually in InDesign. Use the **Reset Position** button that appears after a manual move to snap it back to the configured position on the next redraw.

---

## Legend Layout

- **Column** — Legend items are stacked vertically (default for left/right positions).
- **Row** — Legend items are arranged horizontally (default for top/bottom positions).

The orientation is set automatically when you change the position, but you can override it here.

---

## Legend Alignment

Text alignment within each legend item label: *Left*, *Center*, *Right*, or *Justified*.

## Legend Label Position

Controls whether the label appears before or after its marker and value in each legend item: **First** or **Last**.
