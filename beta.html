Join the ChartStyler beta

ChartStyler will soon be available on the Adobe Marketplace. Before the release, we're testing it with InDesign users. As a beta tester you get early access to the plugin and a direct line to the developer: your feedback decides what gets fixed and improved first.

Sign up for the beta

By signing up you agree to the Beta Program Terms & Conditions below.

What you need

  • Adobe InDesign 2025 or later, on macOS or Windows
  • Some charts to make: real projects are the best test

What we ask

  • Use ChartStyler in your own work and tell us what works and what doesn't
  • Report bugs, and when asked, share a log file or a sample document
  • Install new test versions as they come out

What happens next

After you sign up, we'll email you when there's a place for you in the beta, with the download and installation instructions. In the meantime, the documentation shows how ChartStyler works.
